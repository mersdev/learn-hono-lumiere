Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our curated pieces, ordering process, and authenticity verification system.

Are pieces produced in small batches? ↓

Yes. The catalogue stock is intentionally finite so students and collectors can see the API and checkout guard inventory in real time.

Is this a real payment flow? ↓

No. The lesson stops at a dummy confirmed order and never collects sensitive credit card or banking details.

How does product authentication work? ↓

You can enter your unique serial number (found in your receipt or item documentation) into our Authenticate portal to verify its secure origin against our external ledger database.

Where does the application data live? ↓

Products, user sessions, and verified orders live securely in a Cloudflare D1 database behind a high-performance Hono Worker. The browser only stores your active cart locally.

Can I customize or modify the frontend design? ↓

Absolutely. You can start on your student branch, run milestone checks, and utilize the clean modular structure to tweak components as needed.
